BASSFORGE Support

BASSFORGE is a custom subwoofer enclosure design and build-planning app. You pick your subwoofer and the space you have to work with, and it figures out the box dimensions, cut sheet, materials list, and expected response so you can build the box yourself.

Common questions

Where are my designs saved?

On your device only. BASSFORGE has no accounts and no server, so your designs never leave your phone. If you uninstall the app, your saved designs are deleted along with it.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account, ever. The app works offline — the one feature that uses the internet is the vehicle lookup, which pulls vehicle model lists from NHTSA's public database. You can always enter a vehicle by hand instead.

Why does my design say NOT BUILDABLE?

It means the numbers you entered can't be built in the space you gave. For example, the port the design needs may be longer than the box can hold, or the box volume may not fit the dimensions. It doesn't mean you did anything wrong — that combination just doesn't work as entered.

What does the "Make it buildable" button do?

It starts from your design and adjusts your own settings — things like box size and tuning — toward the closest version that actually fits the space you entered and can be built. The result is checked with the same calculations the app uses for every design. If nothing workable fits your space, it will say so instead of pretending.

Is my subwoofer in the app?

The app includes a catalog of over 600 subwoofers from 18 brands. If yours isn't listed, you can enter its specs yourself — the Thiele/Small parameters from the manufacturer's spec sheet — and design with those.

Does BASSFORGE collect my data?

The app collects no personal information, and there are no accounts, ads, or tracking. The one online lookup — vehicle models from NHTSA — sends only the vehicle type, make, and year you picked, never anything about you. See the BASSFORGE Privacy Policy for details.
