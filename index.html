BASSFORGE Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 21, 2026

BASSFORGE is a custom subwoofer enclosure design and build-planning application.

Information BASSFORGE collects

The current version of BASSFORGE does not require a user account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information or sensitive user data with the developer or third parties.

Design information entered into BASSFORGE is used on the device to calculate enclosure designs and build-planning results. The current version does not transmit this information to a BASSFORGE server.

BASSFORGE does not currently include advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs.

Data sharing

Because the current version does not collect personal or sensitive user data, BASSFORGE does not sell or share such data with third parties.

Data retention and deletion

The current version does not maintain a developer-operated online account or server-side database of user data. Therefore, the developer does not retain personal or sensitive user data from the app on a BASSFORGE server.

Data stored locally by the app, if any, can be removed through normal Android app-data controls or by uninstalling the app.

Security

BASSFORGE is designed to minimize data collection. If future versions introduce online services or data collection, this policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosures will be updated as appropriate.

Children's privacy

BASSFORGE is a technical design and build-planning tool and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. The current version does not knowingly collect personal information from users.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated when BASSFORGE features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
